408
Capacity of the Universe on the Basis of Observer-Function Analysis in Nonlocal Unification
Authors: Chawla, Aman Year: 2026
Journal: REAL Institute | No: assets/Soft_Copies/nuTheoremCapacityv1p2.pdf
We develop an information-theoretic extension of Nonlocal Unification (NU) in which the observer function is interpreted as a communication channel from fundamental informational change to perceived spacetime. The input to this channel is measured explicitly in bits, while the output is not identified with an assumed ontological spacetime but with an observer-dependent perceived spacetime configuration. Since every such configuration is a statistical object, its representational entropy can be measured in bits. We therefore define the capacity of an observer-function channel as the maximum mutual information between informational input and perceived spacetime output, and define a universal capacity by an appropriate supremum over observers. We then introduce noisy observers by allowing the observer function itself to fluctuate. The resulting conditional entropy quantifies uncertainty in perceived spacetime induced specifically by perturbations of the representation function. The principal result of this paper is a supercapacity defect theorem: for an information-sampling rate exceeding the universal channel capacity, the probability of an arbitrarily accurate perceived-spacetime representation cannot be driven to zero by increasing the number of informational channel uses.

From Brownian Motion to Informational Einstein Equations: A Subordinator Perspective
Authors: Chawla, Aman Year: 2026
Journal: REAL Institute | No: assets/soft_copies/aliterNUv2.pdf
We present a conceptual and mathematical bridge from stochastic processes to spacetime geometry. Beginning with Brownian motion and the confinement problem, we show how subordinators emerge as monotone growth processes. We then reinterpret the Einstein Field Equations (EFE) as subordinators of Gaussian matter/energy increments. Finally, we connect this framework to the Theory of Nonlocal Unification (NU), which posits informational EFEs as the unique macroscopic law of fluctuating spacetime.
