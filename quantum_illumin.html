Stroboscope-Guided Quantum Scattering

Authors: A. Chawla

📅 2026 📄 Preprint 🏷️ Status: Zenodo Preprint

Abstract: We propose a dynamic quantum illumination framework designed for tracking and state estimation of non-stationary targets in noisy environments. Building upon foundational principles of quantum illumination, our proposed variant introduces a dynamic enclosure wherein the detection apparatus incorporates simultaneous angular rotation (θ, ϕ) and radial modulation (r). By continuously scanning the return flux landscape, the platform identifies the maximal quantum return flux position to triangulate the highest-probability quantum location of a moving object. Upon optimal trajectory estimation, the spatial coordinates trigger a pulsed, focused Stern-Gerlach particle beam targeted at the estimated interaction site. We ground this mechanism in quantum hypothesis testing, continuous parameter estimation, and optimal quantum field state discrimination, highlighting key advantages over classical radar and stationary entanglement-assisted sensing protocols.

Interferometric Quantum Stroboscope

Authors: A. Chawla

📅 2025 📄 Preprint 🏷️ Status: Zenodo Preprint

Abstract: Quantum illumination (QI) has traditionally been used to detect low-reflectivity objects in noisy environments using entangled photon pairs. In this note, we explore a hybrid setup that combines the phase sensitivity of classical interference with the robustness of quantum illumination. We analyze how the interference at the point of object reflection can be integrated with QI to detect displacements and motion, particularly periodic motion, with enhanced sensitivity. This leads naturally to the concept of a quantum stroboscope, enabling high-frequency motion detection via quantum protocols.
