A High SNR Message From Eternity


A drop of dew, sitting on the precipice
It is ready to drop off and dissolve into the ocean
This life of yours, is similarly placed
It is poised to dissolve into the cosmic life.


A flash of lightning, a thunder clap,
This breath of yours, is your life sap,
Limited in quantity, determining span,
This breath of yours, be grateful for it.


When focus is scattered, or extended down,
Remember to breathe, from the fontanelle up
This single act, a short inhalataion
Refocuses attention away from temptation.

Find the truth, in every way
Known to you, unknowable too
For every truth found this way
Is a nail in night-and-day


Truth fountains showering you
And wisdom rain smothering you
Such is the bounty in eternity
Endless you, everywhere you.
