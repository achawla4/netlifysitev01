Sourcing & Provenance Notice

Every book in the REAL Book Conservatory is sourced used, through legal channels — student donations, campus book drives, and reputable marketplaces (such as reputable used-book marketplaces). We do not acquire from vendors known to circulate photocopied or counterfeit editions.

Before a book is listed for sale, it goes through a documented upgrade process:

  1. Inspection — every copy is checked page-by-page and its condition documented (cover, spine, markings, missing pages).
  2. Restoration — cleaning, spine repair, and supportive rebinding where needed, plus a protective cover.
  3. Cataloguing — ISBN, edition, condition grade, and acquisition source are recorded before the book is listed.

Because of this inspection, restoration, and cataloguing work, listed prices are set above the original used-purchase price — this reflects the labour of conservation, not a markup on the book alone. All titles are available for direct sale and shipping anywhere within India.

Vision & Mission

Vision

The REAL Book Conservatory preserves, restores, curates, and circulates books that advance science, engineering, medicine, mathematics, philosophy, and human flourishing.

Mission

The Conservatory acquires academic and scholarly books from graduating students, researchers, professors, and libraries; restores and catalogues them; and redistributes them to learners, educators, and institutions — supporting the research and educational mission of the REAL Institute. This is stewardship, not retail. Site is operational, but undergoing development.
