REAL's own cosmology reaches further still, toward the inhabitants of the cosmos beyond Earth.

The two sections below are small, honest fragments of that aspiration, not proof of it: a handful of named research collaborators tied to three real nations, and a week of website visitors from four. Neither is the whole nest. Both are real threads of it.

Global Collaboration Network

Every co-authored publication in the REAL Institute's 400-paper table of contents, traced to the nation where each collaborator was based or trained. The overwhelming majority of the corpus is single-authored by Aman Chawla; the cards below reflect the small, real set of papers with named co-authors. Collaborators are identified by initials only.

Method: each of the 400 entries in toc.html was checked for its listed "Authors" field. Co-authored papers were traced to each collaborator's known institutional home at the time of the work. Solo-authored papers (the large majority of the corpus) are not represented here, since they involve no external collaboration.
