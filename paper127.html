Characterization of Cascaded LTI Instances Using Mutual Information: a Computer Study

Chawla, Aman

Abstract

In this note the authors prepare a free space optical channel of 18 cm propagation length and characterize its low frequency behavior. We prepare an FSO communication system of roughly 18 cm propagation length. We use a red laser and a basic LDR. We characterize the FSO channel by transmitting randomly generated bits using Amplitude Modulation at dierent frequencies. We capture the gain between reception and transmission. We also capture the correlation between the transmitted waveform and the received waveform. This is done for: 1. Clear FSO 2. FSO with translucent ruler 3. FSO with translucent taped ruler 4.
