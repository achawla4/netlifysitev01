The following academic and professional achievements provide contextual background for the research, advisory, and analytical activities conducted under the REAL Institute. They are presented as supporting credentials, complementing demonstrated work and outcomes.

Cornell University — Undergraduate Honors (Summa Cum Laude)

Awarded undergraduate honors summa cum laude by Cornell University, recognizing sustained academic excellence and rigorous performance across the undergraduate curriculum.

This distinction reflects a strong formal foundation in analytical reasoning, mathematical methods, and theoretical inquiry, informing the Institute’s emphasis on precision, internal consistency, and disciplined modeling.

IEEE Senior Member

Elevated to the grade of Senior Member by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), a professional distinction conferred through peer review and nomination.

Senior Member status recognizes a minimum of ten years of professional practice and documented evidence of significant technical or scientific performance, indicating sustained engagement with the engineering and research community.

Credentials are presented for transparency and context. The Institute’s work is evaluated primarily through its analyses, research outputs, and practical contributions.
