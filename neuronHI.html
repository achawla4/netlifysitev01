Dramatic Velocity Changes Under Temperature Differentials in Ephaptic Tracts

Authors: [Author Names]

📅 2025 📄 Preprint 🏷️ Status: Zenodo Preprint

Abstract: In this note, the authors present the output of in-silico investigations that demonstrate the dependence of ephaptic conduction profiles on the presence of slight temperature variations amongst the axons in a tract. The results show that spurious spikes and altered conduction velocities are created when a temperature gradient is applied. This has implications for our understanding of nerve conduction in non-homeostatic conditions of altered body temperature.

How This Research Integrates

Neurons are the physical substrate of mind, yet how electrochemical signals become the felt quality of experience remains mysterious. Our research explores whether neuronal activity exhibits quantum properties, and how networks of neurons create the unified field we call consciousness. This bridges neuroscience with the yogic understanding of the subtle body's energy channels (nadis).
