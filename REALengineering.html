The Client Message

Have an AI idea that needs to become a real system?

You do not need another generic outsourcing vendor. You need engineers who can understand the problem, work through the mathematics and architecture, build the prototype, and help turn it into a usable product.

Bring us the difficult part.

REAL Engineering is designed for projects where software development intersects with AI, mathematics, scientific computing, simulation, signal processing or advanced research.

We can work from a specification, research paper, prototype, algorithm, dataset or partially built codebase. Our goal is not to maximize lines of code; it is to produce a technically defensible working result.

Why Gurugram?

India provides a deep engineering talent pool and an attractive cost structure. REAL adds a research-oriented layer: direct senior technical involvement, interdisciplinary problem-solving, and an emphasis on reproducibility and documentation.

US clients can start small: a narrowly defined paid pilot, followed by a larger project or continuing engineering engagement if the collaboration works.
