Computational Substratum Dynamics in Vedic & Para-Munda Dialects

Prajnanabha / Volume 1 Issue 9 / Computational Substratum Dynamics in Vedic & Para-Munda Dialects

Abstract

This monograph presents quantitative phonological n-gram analyses of acoustic and structural transitions in ancient Indian canonical texts. Focusing on the interaction between Austroasiatic/Para-Munda substrate dialects and Early Vedic Sanskrit during the Late Harappan period (c. 1900–1200 BCE), we track retroflex consonant assimilation and prefixation shifts.
