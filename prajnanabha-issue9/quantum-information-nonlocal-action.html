Quantum Information Systems & Nonlocal Action Mechanics

Prajnanabha / Volume 1 Issue 9 / Quantum Information Systems & Nonlocal Action Mechanics

Abstract

This paper formulates a continuous information theory framework that unifies Brownian stochastic motion with quantum measurement theory. By modeling state trajectory reduction as gradient descent over an informational Free Energy Action functional, we derive continuous entropy bounds for non-equilibrium quantum systems.

Key mathematical results establish that the atypical subspace error bounds in quantum source coding mirror the thermodynamic entropy production in stochastic non-local physical systems.
