Biophysical Neural Coding & Ephaptic Field Coherence

Prajnanabha / Volume 1 Issue 9 / Biophysical Neural Coding & Ephaptic Field Coherence

Abstract

We analyze dendritic arbor geometry and axonal field coupling using incidence geometry and the joints theorem. Bounded-degree incidence graphs provide strict upper bounds on branching complexity, establishing that synaptic pruning optimizes information capacity against ephaptic field interference.
