Applications & Screening Notice

This first iteration of the seminar is offered, free of cost, to a limited number of participants selected through a strict screening and application process. Seats are few; sincerity and readiness matter more than qualifications.

  1. Apply — the application, via Google Forms, is openend on Guru Purnima day, 29 July 2026, and stays open until seats are filled.
  2. Screen — every application is reviewed for eligibility, sincerity, and readiness for spiritual and common-discipline; some applicants may be contacted for a short conversation.
  3. Selected — selected participants are informed directly and their postal address confirmed for courier delivery of materials.
  4. Materials Arrive — all study materials are couriered to each selected participant's home, one week ahead of the seminar, i.e. by 24 November 2026.

The Google Forms link is above, on this page and also will be shared through the Institute's channels from Guru Purnima day onward. Applying does not guarantee selection.

Charter

Vision & Mission

Vision

To carry forward, in the lineage of Gurudev Paramahamsa Hariharananda Giri, a discipline of self-upliftment in which each young man learns to be his own truest friend — steady, sincere, and unafraid of the inner work.

Mission

The key message of this seminar is self-discipline — of the self, by the self, for the self — in keeping with the teaching Sri Krishna gave on the battlefield of Kurukshetra, in what is today Haryana, the very state where this first iteration of the seminar is conducted online:

uddharet ātmanā ātmānaṁ nātmānam avasādayet
ātmaiva hyātmano bandhur ātmaiva ripur ātmanaḥ

In plain words: uplift yourself by your own self; do not let yourself sink. The self alone is the friend of the self, and the self alone is its enemy. This seminar exists to help each participant discover that friend within, through spirituality, self-discipline, and the common-discipline that binds a life of practice together.

What's Inside

What the Seminar Covers

Six days, entirely online, built around three threads that reinforce one another — spirituality, self-discipline, and common-discipline.

01 · Spirituality

Learning & Practising

  • Foundations of spiritual life in the Kriya Yoga lineage
  • Guided sessions of sitting, silence, and reflection
  • Space for genuine questions, not performance
02 · Self-Discipline

The Inner Practice

  • Daily habits that steady body, breath, and mind
  • Working directly with the Gita's teaching on the self
  • Practical structure to carry beyond the six days
03 · Common-Discipline

Living Alongside Others

  • Shared norms of conduct, punctuality, and courtesy
  • Discipline as something practised together, not alone
  • Preparing participants to hold a shared standard
04 · Delivery

Fully Online

  • Live sessions, 1–6 December 2026
  • Medium of instruction: English
  • Hindi translation available throughout
05 · Materials

Couriered, Not Downloaded

  • Printed study materials mailed to selected participants
  • Delivered to their home address one week in advance
  • No cost to the participant at any stage
06 · Cost

Entirely Free

  • No fee for the seminar or the materials
  • No fee for courier delivery
  • Offered as seva, in the spirit of the lineage

Eligibility Criteria

This first iteration is deliberately narrow in scope, so the Institute can screen closely and support each participant well. All of the following must be true.

1
Single Male

Applicant must be an unmarried male.

2
Aged 18–45

Between 18 and 45 years of age at the time of application.

3
Sincere Aspirant

Genuinely desirous of learning and practising spirituality, self-discipline, and common-discipline.

4
Sound Health

Of sound physical and mental health, able to participate fully in a six-day online seminar.

5
Available for Screening

Willing and available to go through the Institute's application and screening process in full.
