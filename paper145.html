Simulating Nanofluidics: an LLM Study

Chawla, Aman

Abstract

In this note the authors present initial results that show the magnetic eld and its power spectrum generated by a box of N particles with ran- dom velocities and a drift. The simulation is intended to mimic a chunk of uid in motion, with the goal of determining its velocity using an NV center (Nitrogen Vacancy) which can sense the magnetic eld. In the paper [1], the authors present molecular dynamics simulations to mimic uid ow and then sense the ow using an NV center via the magnetic eld generated. Their analysis shows that the power spectrum of the measured mag- netic eld is related to the drift velocity of the uid. In this note, we use an LLM to present us with a Python script (Algorithm 1-2) and simulation results (Figures 1 and 2) for this setting. In future work, we will provide a specic bounding box shape and study ow in it using similar methodology. Our goal is ultimately to develop a nanopump that can generate nanoows which can then be sensed using the mechanism simulated in this note.
