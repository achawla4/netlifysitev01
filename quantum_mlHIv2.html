On the Quantum Biophysics of Deep Learning

लेखक: A. Chawla

📅 २०२५ 📄 प्रीप्रिंट 🏷️ स्थिति: Zenodo प्रीप्रिंट

सार: We propose an extension of the quantum perceptron framework into a multi-layered structure, creating a formalism for quantum deep learning. Each layer consists of entangled motional states processed via environment-induced voltage steps that execute projection, summation, and activation. The recursive imprinting of environmental patterns across such layers potentially underlies robust, field-driven cognition in nature. Applications range from fault-detection and memory abstraction to linguistic character recognition.

A Quantum Extension of Action Potential Coupling in ३D Tracts

लेखक: A. Chawla

📅 २०२४ 📄 प्रीप्रिंट 🏷️ स्थिति: Zenodo प्रीप्रिंट

सार: In this note the authors consider ions in nodes on different axons in a ३D axon bundle. The ions are proposed to be studied quantum mechanically, with their evolution under gravitational waves and a bath, being coupled via their field to distant ions in nodes on other axons within the same tract. The setup is closely linked with the quantum perceptron proposed earlier by the authors. The expected novel result is a ३D formulation of the १D Schrodinger Equation in the coupled setting.

यह अनुसंधान कैसे समेकित होता है

Quantum machine learning explores whether quantum phenomena underlie learning and intelligence itself. If consciousness emerges from quantum processes in biological systems, then quantum algorithms may reveal fundamental principles of awareness. This bridges neuroscience, AI, and contemplative traditions that view mind as a quantum-like field of potential.
