The Institute for Road Design is a seva initiative of REAL dedicated to the scientific design and layout of roads, and to the technology used in their construction. This page lists upcoming publications and provides a space to add them as they are released.

Institute for Road Design

A REAL Seva Initiative

Roads carry more than traffic — they carry the daily life, safety, and economic movement of entire communities. The Institute for Road Design serves this quiet, essential infrastructure by studying the scientific principles behind road geometry and layout, and by tracking the technologies that make road construction safer, more durable, and more efficient. This is offered in the spirit of seva: disinterested service toward work that benefits the many.

See Upcoming Publications

Scope of Study

🗺

Geometric Design & Layout

Alignment, curvature, gradient, sight distance, intersection layout, and the underlying scientific principles that determine how a road should be shaped for safety and efficiency.

Where mathematics meets the landscape

🔧

Construction Technology

Materials science, pavement engineering, earthworks, and the construction methods and equipment that turn a road design into durable, real-world infrastructure.

Where design meets the ground

🌐

Safety & Sustainability

Traffic engineering, road safety audits, drainage, durability under climate stress, and sustainable materials and practices for long-term road performance.

Roads built to serve, not just to last

📚

Publications & Standards

Review and synthesis of scientific literature, technical standards, and applied research relevant to road design and construction practice.

Bringing scattered knowledge together
