The Perfect Feedback Exponent is Not Achieved Everywhere by Every Asymptoting Two Phase Noisy Feedback Scheme

Chawla, Aman

Abstract

In this brief note, we present a result pertaining to the bounds on the error exponent of a continuous time gaussian channel with noisy feedback. In the SM thesis [1], a simple two phase scheme is presented for the noisy gaussian feedback setting in continuous time. The achieved reliability function is lower bounded there. Here we make use of a bound from [2, 3]. It is an upper bound on the perfect feedback reliability function.
