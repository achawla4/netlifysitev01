यह अनुसंधान कैसे समेकित होता है

Quantum supercomputers leverage superposition—existing in many states at once—to solve problems intractable for classical machines. Similarly, meditative consciousness can hold multiple perspectives simultaneously without collapsing into dualistic thinking. Both quantum and contemplative states transcend binary logic, accessing a dimension where "both/and" replaces "either/or."
