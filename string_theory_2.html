Modulator Design in String Information Theory

लेखक: A. Chawla

📅 २०२५ 📄 प्रीप्रिंट 🏷️ स्थिति: Zenodo प्रीप्रिंट

सार: We explore a novel way for information and charge transport in string theory, involving stochastic open-closed transitions of strings interacting with D-branes. Field strings undergoing such transitions act as directional filters, enabling the transport of charged mass strings in a preferred direction. By using a dual filter system and a diffusion mechanism, we demonstrate the emergence of sustained oscillations and charge separation. The resulting dipole field exhibits amplitude modulation, suggesting a string-theoretic realization of radiative signaling via emergent electrodynamic-like behavior. We construct the relevant mathematical model with detailed derivations.

यह अनुसंधान कैसे समेकित होता है

String theory proposes that all particles are vibrating strings—reality as music. This scientific framework mirrors the Vedic teaching of Nada Brahma: the universe as primordial sound. Both describe existence not as static matter, but as dynamic resonance. The mathematics of string theory may be describing what yogis experience as the unstruck sound (anahata nada) in deep meditation.
