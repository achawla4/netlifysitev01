How This Research Integrates

Physics seeks to unify the four fundamental forces into one equation—the Theory of Everything. This mirrors the spiritual realization of advaita (non-duality): all apparent diversity emerging from one undifferentiated source. Both science and spirituality pursue the same goal: understanding how multiplicity arises from unity, and recognizing that separation is illusion.
