Data & Storage

Transparent local file architecture.

Your data remains entirely on your machine in open, structured formats.

Local Profile Directory

Structured Notepad v3 saves all documents and embedded media automatically as structured JSON files within your local profile directory:

C:\Users\<YourUsername>\StructuredNotepadDocs\

Backups & Portability: To transfer notes to another machine or maintain regular backups, copy the entire StructuredNotepadDocs folder to any drive or storage medium.
