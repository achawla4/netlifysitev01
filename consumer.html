Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Hariharananda REAL Institute ("REAL Institute," "we," "us," or "our") operates the official websites yogoreal.net and realinstitute.netlify.app (the "Site"), through which we share our research, publications, software suites, Kriya Yoga archive, and Book Conservatory items, and through which visitors may place orders, make voluntary offerings, or submit inquiries. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide directly: Name, delivery email address, phone/WhatsApp number (for physical shipments), shipping address, and order transaction reference numbers (such as UPI UTR or PayPal Transaction ID).
  • Payment information: Transactions are completed securely via standard Domestic UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) or International PayPal (debit/credit card processing). We do not store your credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Automatically collected information: Technical diagnostics such as browser type, IP country, visited pages, and referral sources logged by our secure hosting infrastructure (Netlify Edge) to ensure Site availability and security.
  • Third-party links & tools: Materials, research papers, and affiliate recommendations linked to external platforms (such as GitHub or Google Drive) operate under their respective privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process and deliver digital access links (e.g., Kriya Corpus recordings, Leibnitz software licenses, digital publications) to your verified email address within 24 hours.
  • To fulfill physical book orders and dispatch shipments to your designated postal address.
  • To respond to inquiries, prayer requests, seva interest, research proposals, and initiation requests.
  • To maintain security and prevent unauthorized activity on our public services.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared strictly:

  • with payment processors and banking infrastructure (e.g., PhonePe, PayPal, FormSubmit) solely to verify order completion;
  • with postal/courier partners to deliver physical goods to your shipping location; or
  • when required by law or formal legal process under applicable regulations.

4. Data Retention & Rights

We retain order verification logs and correspondence for as long as necessary to fulfill delivery, resolve customer service inquiries, or satisfy legal and tax accounting obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or removal of your personal data by writing to aman.chawla@gmail.com.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy outlines your rights regarding cancellations, refunds, and replacements under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and global e-commerce standards.

1. Digital Products & Access Links

If a digital deliverable (such as a Kriya Corpus access link or software download) cannot be delivered, fails technical verification, or is unfulfilled due to an issue on our end, you are entitled to a **100% full refund** or immediate re-delivery upon request. Refund requests for unfulfilled digital orders are processed within 24-48 hours.

2. Physical Goods

For physical books sold directly by REAL Institute, if an item arrives damaged, defective, or fails to deliver, we will provide a free replacement or a full refund upon notification within 14 days of receipt.

3. Voluntary Offerings & Donations

Voluntary general donations to support our research mission are non-refundable. However, if a contribution was executed in error (such as a duplicate transaction), please contact us within 7 days for a prompt review and full refund.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email aman.chawla@gmail.com with your name, order details, transaction reference (UTR or PayPal ID), and reason for request. Refunds are returned to the original payment source (UPI account or PayPal card balance).
