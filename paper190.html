Some Progress on Axonal Quantum Computation in the Markin Approximation

Chawla, Aman

Abstract

In this note, the authors consider Markin's approximation to the ionic current, in the context of the Frankenhaeuser-Huxley equations. Using a Green's function approach, they derive the solution to the equations. This is a rst step in a program to determine the exact quantum computation sequence in axon tracts. It is well recognized that the Hodgkin Huxley equations or the corresponding Frankenhaeuser-Huxley equations are dicult to solve exactly due to the com- plexity of the ionic current term [1]. Markin and co-authors circumvent this diculty by assuming a general form of the ionic current [2, 3]. In a paper by Markin and Chizmadzhev cited within [1], they present a piecewise-linear approach to the ion channel current. In later work, Markin uses this formulation to solve the single and multiple axon coupling situation. In Section 1, we set the problem up and then use the Green's method for solving the two-variable second order partial dierential equation that arises. We conclude in Section 2. Problem Setup and Solution We utilize the Markin model for the ion currents. They can be succinctly written using Heaviside step functions. In particular, let I(z, t) stand for the ionic current at a time t at a node.
