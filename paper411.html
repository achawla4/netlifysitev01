Abstract

Axonal bundles subjected to high-frequency electrical fields play a pivotal role in modern bioelectronic medicine and deep-brain stimulation. Classical linear tridiagonal solvers (such as the Thomas algorithm) struggle under non-stationary, fractional Fourier domain (FrFT) modulation and phase-space dynamics. Here, we present an adaptive FrFT-Thomas framework that converts tridiagonal elimination into symplectic phase-space sweeps. By operating in the optimal fractional domain, the algorithm preserves phase-space volume, eliminates numerical dissipation, and scales linearly O(N) for large-scale axonal tract simulations.
