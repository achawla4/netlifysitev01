Mahasiddhartha About • This manual talks about the Great Attainment, the Highest End of Any Being in Any Realm. • The Great Attainment may be symbolized by Om. A System of Truths • 1. Soul and life are inseparable • 2. Nature of soul is bliss • 3. This bliss is beyond mind • 4. It is the bliss of knowing • 5. Soul and life can be unified in subjective perception • 6. Meditation is the unification of soul and life • 7. When meditation becomes natural, it is the Great Attainment • 8. The system of truths merges in the ocean of truth A System of Meditation • Meditation on the system of truths • Detachment from all states Advanced Meditation • 1. It requires harmonization between inner and outer • 2. It requires renunciation of ambition and pleasure • 3. With 1. and 2. meditation is made natural Fruits of the Great Attainment • The limited frame of man becomes a blessing to all the realms. And he himself the sole, one Consciousness. Rules for Daily Living • 1. Daily contemplation on the manual, at every opportunity • 2. Celibacy • 3. Service to society in any way • 4. Reverence to every being as a Mahasidhhaartha
