Information Theory Fundamentals and Core

Mathematical foundations of information, entropy, and their applications to consciousness studies

About This Course

This comprehensive course by Hariharananda REAL Institute explores the mathematical foundations of information theory, from basic probability concepts to advanced applications in consciousness studies. Through about 20 detailed lectures, you'll develop a deep understanding of entropy, coding theory, and the quantitative nature of information.

Prerequisites: Basic calculus and probability theory
